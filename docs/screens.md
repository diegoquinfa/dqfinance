# DQFinance — Screens

What each route holds. The design system itself (tokens, type, layout, rules) is in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md);
the component catalogue is in [components.md](./components.md).

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | The landing: the lockup, the language, sign in and create an account in the top bar (on a phone the top bar keeps only sign in, and the hero carries the create-account call), with a lilac scroll line under it; a full-height hero over the animated dot grid, with the two-tone statement, a paragraph, the two calls to action ("Try the demo" and "Create an account") and a live ledger whose total recomputes as movements arrive; 01 how it works, one large paragraph that lights up word by word with the scroll; 02 your assistant, connected: a scripted chat in the assistant's own app (Grok, Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini), the MCP line between that app and DQFinance, a two-tone "no chatbot of our own" statement, the three connection steps as an animated timeline and the guest note; 03 what it does, as a ruled grid of six animated drawings with one line each; 04 the screens, as three tabs beside a screenshot, pinned while the scroll moves from one tab to the next; 05 make it yours: open source on Cloudflare, with the stack, "Deploy to Cloudflare", the code link and a terminal typing the deploy; a closing call to action over the dot grid; then the footer with the repository links. A signed-in visitor goes to the overview instead. Motion rules: DESIGN.md, Motion. |
| `/sign-in` | Two-tone statement and the sign-in panel: email and password, a link to create an account, and a "forgot your password" link only when the server can send a reset link. Also the OAuth login page for MCP clients, whose signed query travels with the links to the other pages. Language picker inside the panel. |
| `/sign-up` | The sign-up panel: name, email, password and its repeat, which the client checks before sending. It creates the account, signs the user in, and continues an OAuth flow when there is one. |
| `/forgot-password` | The reset panel: an email address, then the same answer whether or not the address has an account. |
| `/reset-password` | The new password and its repeat, then a link back to sign in. Without a good token it says the link expired and offers a new one. |
| `/consent` | OAuth consent: the agent's name, then each permission as a row. Deny is `outline`, Allow is primary. A guest never gets here: the card says a guest cannot connect an agent and offers to create an account. |
| `/onboarding` | Two steps (country / language / timezone, then currencies) in the same layout as sign-in. |
| `/overview` | The summary band: 01 total balance (display size, primary currency) with its other currencies and any in-transit amounts, beside the period block (Week / Month / Year with previous and next, then Spent, Received and Net, summed by `GET /api/transactions/summary`). Then "To confirm" only when it has items; the sections below take the next free number, in this order. This month: the forecast in 7 of 12 columns, over the whole month: what the accounts held at the end of each day so far (`ink`, solid), then from today the projected balance of each day until the end of the month (`signal`, dashed over a hatch), with each statement leaving on its payment day; a dotted line marks today, the last day carries its projected amount, and a legend names Actual and Forecast. The day it is lowest is named above the chart and the daily pace it spends from tomorrow on under the title. Beside it in 5 of 12, the safe-to-spend card: how much may I spend today from what the accounts hold, the bills and card payments still due and the days left, with the monthly target that caps it. Net worth: the per-currency figures (assets, card debt, net) in 5 of 12 columns, beside the month-end assets and liabilities chart in 7 with 3M / 12M. Accounts and cards: the accounts in 7 of 12 columns, beside the credit cards in 5 (rows open the source), each with its cut and payment day, what its statement still asks for ("Paid" once it is, and "Paid X of Y" after a partial payment), what was charged this cycle, and "Add the statement" (or "Edit") opening the statement dialog: the payment, the cut date and the payment date, prefilled from the card; the accounts' "View all" stays in the section rail. Recent movements, 8 of them. |
| `/accounts` | 01 By bank: each bank as a subheading with its "Add account →" link, then its products as rows that open their detail, with the bank account's last 4 as mono metadata; cards and pockets nested with `↳`, a pocket tagged "Pocket" with its rate and payout, and a product with pockets showing "Total with pockets" in mono; each product row has one "Add ▾" menu (Card, Pocket); a bank account with no products says "No products" and offers "Add product →"; then 02 Cash. Every create action is a link to a page, on every screen size. |
| `/accounts/new` | The full-page create form: back link, title, and Create at the top and at the end. 01 Saldo: the opening balance, the large first field, with the currency beside it. 02 Cuenta bancaria: one bank autocomplete (a name that matches one of the user's banks reuses it, any other name creates one), the bank account to use when that bank already has one, and last 4 for a new bank account. 03 Producto: the type as chips, the optional name, and the annual rate. The opening balance is recorded as an adjustment dated today (D2), then the new product's detail opens. |
| `/accounts/new-card` | The card as a page: 01 Tarjeta opens with the live card face and then its kind, the product it belongs to, its name and last 4; 02 Datos de la tarjeta holds the brand, the color and, on a credit card, the interest rate with MV / EA, the limit and the cut and payment days. A credit card opens its own detail, a debit card its product. |
| `/accounts/new-pocket` | The pocket as a page: the account it lives in, read-only, then 01 Monto, the amount set aside now (large, optional, in the parent's currency), and 02 Bolsillo with the name and the annual rate with Daily / Monthly chips that appear once a rate is typed. The amount is recorded as a transfer from the parent, then the pocket's detail opens. |
| `/accounts/$type/$id` | A product, cash wallet or credit card. The summary band: back link, bank, name, balance (or debt and available credit), a credit card's face, and the action row with "Adjust balance", edit, and a pocket's two transfer buttons ("Set aside more", "Return to {parent}"). 01 Income and expenses: spent, received and net for the last 6 or 12 months in 5 of 12 columns, with the period toggle in the section rail, beside the same chart for that source in 7. 02 Returns and costs: a product's yield (rate, "≈ per month" and "≈ per day", or the single figure that matches its payout) in 5 columns beside its costs in 7 — the recurring expenses paid with it — each under a mono subheading, with "Add cost" beside the costs subheading; an account with no rate shows only the costs. 03 Movements, with "View all", and 04 Documents (contract, terms, fee table) with upload, download and delete, both full width. |
| `/transactions` | A search field (the title, the description and the category), then filters (type, source, date range) and the movement list. The filters live in the URL, so a link can preselect a source. On desktop all filters sit in one row; on mobile the type stays inline and a sliders icon beside the title opens a sheet with the source and dates, each active filter showing as a chip that clears it. The list is grouped under a heading per day; each row shows its bank (and contact) muted beside the title, then its category and account in the mono line; the source drops out when a source filter is on. Tapping a row, in this and every movement list, opens its detail sheet (amount, date, source, category, contact, description) with Edit, which swaps in the prefilled form, and Delete, which asks first; the fee and FX entries of a transfer show their details only. "Import" opens the statement import: on mobile it sits beside the page title, since the bottom bar holds the new-movement action. |
| `/transactions/import` | 01 Statement: the source, the pasted rows or a chosen .csv file, and the assistant note. 02 Columns: the four column picks, shown once the statement has headers. 03 Preview: the dry-run table with duplicates marked and left out, rows that only look like one marked as possible duplicates and still imported, and "Import N movements" in the section rail. PDFs and screenshots are imported by asking the AI assistant. |
| `/transfers` | 01 All transfers: each side leads with its bank, or with its own name when it has no bank (a cash wallet), with the account's masked last 4 below, a derived type tag, fee and FX notes, an "in transit" tag, "Mark received", and Delete, which asks first. |
| `/recurring` | 01 Due now: an editable amount, Confirm and Skip. 02 Monthly plan: the full schedule in 7 of 12 columns beside the recurring incomes in 5, each under a mono subheading with its total a month under it, so a weekly charge counts 52/12 and a yearly one a twelfth; the new-income action sits beside the incomes subheading. The new form asks for the first charge date, the day the expense falls due the first time; the later charges repeat from it. Both new forms take the contact that pays or receives it the same way: a name already there is reused, a new one becomes a contact. |
| `/calendar` | 01 the month (previous / next, both in the URL with the chosen day): spent, received and still due in the primary currency, then a Monday-first grid where each day shows its spent (`−`), received (`+`, `income`) and `↻` for recurring expenses due, as compact amounts from `sm` and as dots below it; today's number is `signal`. 02 the chosen day (today by default in the current month): its recurring expenses due in 5 of 12 columns beside its movements in 7, each under a mono subheading, with View all beside the due heading; when only one of the two has rows it takes the full width. Days are the user's timezone; transfers and adjustments never count (D3). |
| `/contacts` | 01 Compare: each row's "Compare" adds that contact to the chart and turns into "Remove", the chosen ones (kept in the URL as `compare`) are drawn month by month in a pastel tone each with their total in ink, and the block carries an Expenses / Incomes switch and a period (6M / 12M) — the reason to stop opening two windows. 02 All contacts: one list with a kind filter (All / People / Businesses) and a search, both in the URL; every row carries a PERSONA or COMERCIO tag, the money spent and received, and how many movements it has and when the last one was; the row menu renames or deletes the contact. |
| `/contacts/$id` | One contact: its name, its kind, its totals and how many movements it has and when, a "New movement" action that opens the dialog with that contact already chosen, a link back to the list with its filters, then 01 Breakdown: the categories (each with a bar of its share) in 5 of 12 columns beside the monthly spending line in 7, with the period (6M / 12M) in that section's rail; and 02 Movements, with a "View all" link for the ones past the first fifty. |
| `/settings` | Language, currencies, categories (each with its mark; rename it, give it an icon and a color, delete one that no movement, recurring expense or budget uses, or merge one into another and the target's limit wins when both have one), notifications (Web Push, the only permission prompt), password, the webhooks block (the URL the app calls when a bill falls due, the secret it signs with, and how many days before it warns; with zero days it never calls), session. |
| `/savings` | 01 Goals: each with what it holds, what is left, what it asks for every month, a progress bar, an action to add what was put in and one to delete the goal. The new goal asks what it is for, how much it aims at and when it should be reached. |
| `/incomes` | 01 Incomes: the money the user expects, each with its amount, how often it arrives, the day the next one lands and the contact who pays it, with a delete that asks first. The new income asks what it is, how much it brings, how often it arrives, when the next one does and who pays it: a contact name that is already there is reused, and a new one becomes a contact when the form is sent. |
| `/budgets` | 01 Monthly limits: a limit per category, each row showing the category and its mark, the limit, what was spent, what is left and "close" when a fifth of it or less is left, over a hairline progress bar. A limit is added through a dialog and deleted with a confirmation. |

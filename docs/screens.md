# DQFinance — Screens

What each route holds. The design system itself (tokens, components, rules) is in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md).

| Route | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `/sign-in` | Two-tone statement and the sign-in panel. Also the OAuth login page for MCP clients. Language picker inside the panel. |
| `/consent` | OAuth consent: the agent's name, then each permission as a row. Deny is `outline`, Allow is primary. |
| `/onboarding` | Two steps (country / language / timezone, then currencies) in the same layout as sign-in. |
| `/overview` | 01 total balance (display size, primary currency) beside the period block: Week / Month / Year with previous and next, then Spent and Received (summed by `GET /api/transactions/summary`). 02 net worth per currency (assets, card debt, net), then the month-end assets and liabilities chart with 3M / 12M. 03 accounts, 04 credit cards (rows open the source), 05 to confirm, 06 recent movements, 07 in transit. |
| `/accounts` | One section per bank: the bank name as the heading, one "Add account →" link, and its products as rows that open their detail, with the bank account's last 4 as mono metadata; cards and pockets nested with `↳`, a pocket tagged "Pocket" with its rate and payout, and a product with pockets showing "Total with pockets" in mono; each product row has one "Add ▾" menu (Card, Pocket); a bank account with no products says "No products" and offers "Add product →"; then cash. Every create action is a link to a page, on every screen size. |
| `/accounts/new` | The full-page create form: back link, title, and Create at the top and at the end. The opening balance is the large first field with the currency beside it, then one bank autocomplete (a name that matches one of the user's banks reuses it, any other name creates one), the bank account to use when that bank already has one, the type as chips, the optional name, last 4 for a new bank account, and the annual rate. The opening balance is recorded as an adjustment dated today (D2), then the new product's detail opens. |
| `/accounts/new-card` | The card as a page: the live card face at the top, then kind, product, name, last 4, brand, interest rate with MV / EA, color and the credit details. A credit card opens its own detail, a debit card its product. |
| `/accounts/new-pocket` | The pocket as a page: the account it lives in, read-only, then the amount set aside now (large, optional, in the parent's currency), the name, and the annual rate with Daily / Monthly chips that appear once a rate is typed. The amount is recorded as a transfer from the parent, then the pocket's detail opens. |
| `/accounts/$type/$id` | A product, cash wallet or credit card: back link, bank, name, balance (or debt and available credit), a credit card's face, "Adjust balance", edit, income / expenses / net for the last 6 or 12 months with the same chart for that source, a product's yield (rate, "≈ per month" and "≈ per day", or the single figure that matches its payout), a pocket's "Move money" block ("Set aside more", "Return to {parent}"), its costs (the recurring expenses paid with it, with "Add cost"), its recent movements with "View all", and its documents (contract, terms, fee table) with upload, download and delete. |
| `/transactions` | Filters (type, source, date range) and the movement list. The filters live in the URL, so a link can preselect a source. On desktop all filters sit in one row; on mobile the type stays inline and a sliders icon beside the title opens a sheet with the source and dates, each active filter showing as a chip that clears it. The list is grouped under a heading per day; each row shows its bank (and contact) muted beside the title, then its category and account in the mono line; the source drops out when a source filter is on. Tapping a row, in this and every movement list, opens its detail sheet (amount, date, source, category, contact, description) with Edit, which swaps in the prefilled form, and Delete, which asks first; the fee and FX entries of a transfer show their details only. "Import" opens the statement import: on mobile it sits beside the page title, since the bottom bar holds the new-movement action. |
| `/transactions/import` | A CSV statement: source, the pasted rows or a chosen .csv file, the column map, then the dry-run preview as a table, duplicates marked and left out, and "Import N movements". PDFs and screenshots are imported by asking the AI assistant. |
| `/transfers` | Transfers where each side leads with its bank, or with its own name when it has no bank (a cash wallet), with the account's masked last 4 below, a derived type tag, fee and FX notes, an "in transit" tag, and "Mark received". |
| `/recurring` | "Due now" with an editable amount, Confirm and Skip, then the full schedule. The new form asks for the first charge date, the day the expense falls due the first time; the later charges repeat from it. |
| `/contacts` | One list of contacts, with a kind filter (All / People / Businesses) and a search, both in the URL. Every row carries a PERSONA or COMERCIO tag, the money spent and received, and how many movements it has and when the last one was; the row menu renames or deletes the contact. |
| `/contacts/$id` | One contact: its name, its kind, its totals and how many movements it has and when, a "New movement" action that opens the dialog with that contact already chosen, a period (6M / 12M) that shapes the two sections below it, then 01 Categories (each category with a bar of its share), 02 Evolution (spending per month as a line), and 03 Movements, with a "View all" link for the ones past the first fifty and a link back to the list with its filters. |
| `/settings` | Language, currencies, categories, notifications (Web Push, the only permission prompt), session. |
